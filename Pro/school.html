Our Story

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Founded in 1985, Vidyalaya Higher Secondary School has stood as a beacon of quality education in the region. We blend rigorous academics with holistic development to create well-rounded individuals ready for the challenges of tomorrow.

Our state-of-the-art campus, experienced faculty, and student-centric approach ensure every learner thrives academically, creatively, and socially.

Our Vision

To be the leading school empowering students to become global citizens and lifelong learners.
